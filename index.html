    Stream

    Ready to broadcast.
    No designer needed.

    Build whole scenes the size of your stream and add them to OBS. Every change is live instantly.

    Create TV broadcastOne click and the finished scene is ready: leaderboard, battle, session, driver in focus and events in 1920 × 1080.
    Same size as OBSThe canvas has exactly your stream's resolution, for example 1920 × 1080. What you see here is what your audience sees.
    Show duringEach scene only appears in the session types you tick. Different in practice than in the race.
    Place freelyArrange every element by dragging and resize it at the corner handle. With Shift in steps of 10.
    LayersTop is in front. The eye hides an element only in the editor, in OBS it stays visible.
    Add to OBSCopy the address, paste it into OBS as a browser source, done. The background is transparent, changes appear instantly.

    Radio

    Radio messages
    on screen.

    Whoever speaks in the iRacing voice chat is shown, just like in Formula 1. When you speak, your profile picture appears.

    • Drivers, team radio and race control, each in its own colour
    • Position, car number and country of the speaker
    • Three sizes: compact, wide and mini

    OBS

    Scenes switch
    by themselves.

    For every situation in iRacing you choose an OBS scene. OM Pitwall switches accordingly while you drive.

    • iRacing not started, garage and pits, on track, replay, checkered flag and optionally pit stop
    • No flicker: switching only happens after an adjustable delay
    • If you switch in OBS yourself, automation pauses for a few minutes
    • Optionally only while streaming or recording, and switchable with a Stream Deck key

    Stream info

    Viewers on YouTube and Twitch, likes, subscribers, followers and uptime. Plus bitrate, dropped frames and CPU load from OBS.

    Stream events

    Subs, raids, bits, Super Chats and new members pop up briefly. Plus the live chat as its own element.

    Stream Deck

    Switch layouts, lock overlays, toggle OBS automation, flip PIT Monitor pages and show values, right from the Elgato Stream Deck.

    PIT Monitor

    The pits.
    On your second screen.

    Timing table with all classes, sectors and pit stops. Arrange any overlay around it as an element, on as many pages as you like.

    1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
    1. 1

      Pages

      Several pages, e.g. race, strategy and replay. When driving and in replay the right page switches by itself; flip pages with the Stream Deck.

    2. 2

      Classes

      GTP, LMP2 and GT3 separated, each with its own leader and strength of field.

    3. 3

      You, always in focus

      Your row is always highlighted. Clicking a driver switches the iRacing camera to them.

    4. 4

      Sectors in colour

      Purple is the fastest time in the field, green a personal best.

    5. 5

      Box, OUT and DNF

      Who is in the pits, on the out lap or retired is shown right after the name.

    6. 6

      Trackmap

      All cars live on track, with turn numbers. In replay the camera car is highlighted.

    7. 7

      Rejoin

      Where you would rejoin after a stop this lap. Calculated from the measured pit lane time loss and your stationary time.

    8. 8

      Race history

      Positions or gap to the class leader over the whole race, with pit stops and retirements. Also when spectating.

    Every overlay as an element

    Fuel calculator, stint plan, weather or Circle of Doom: every overlay can be placed in the PIT Monitor, with its own settings per page.

    OBS picture

    The live picture from OBS via the virtual camera, smooth like a webcam. Keep an eye on your stream from the pits.

    Dividers

    Lines between the elements, glowing if you like. They join into corners and T-pieces by themselves.

    Stint Planner

    24 hours.
    Fully calculated.

    Enter race length, consumption and drivers. OM Pitwall plans stints, stops, tyres and driver changes. And keeps calculating live during the race.

    Enter once

    Set race length, lap time, consumption and tank size, enter drivers. OM Pitwall calculates the rest.

    • Stints, stops and fuel amounts
    • Tyre change every second stop or as you choose
    • Assign drivers freely per stint

    Calculated live

    The live bar shows stint, driver at the wheel, next stop and the next driver change.

    • «Versus plan» in laps
    • Log damage, penalty or spin as an event
    • All following stints shift

    Always in view

    The «Stint plan» overlay shows the active plan in game, in the stream and in the PIT Monitor.

    • Next stop with lap and fuel amount
    • Next driver change
    • Notice when the wrong driver is driving

    Share with the team

    One link for editing, one for viewing. End-to-end encrypted, no account.

    When things change

    Log damage, penalty or spin as an event. All following stints shift.
